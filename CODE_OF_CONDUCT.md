# Code of Conduct

Glass UI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/lemuayala). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove
comments, close threads or block participants who break these rules.
